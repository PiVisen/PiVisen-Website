Privacy notice

Privacy is one of our core values, so this page is short and in plain English. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by PiVisen AI, a technology company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. You can reach us at info@pivisen.com or +92 302 0022779.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company (optional), the topic you pick and your message. We also receive the address of the page you sent it from.
  • Email and phone: whatever you include when you write to us or call us directly.

How your message reaches us

The contact form sends your message through EmailJS, an email delivery service, to our company inbox. EmailJS handles the message only to deliver it to us.

How we use it

We use your details to read your message, reply to you and, if you want, discuss your project. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts are stored on our own server, so loading the site does not contact Google Fonts. Like any website host, our hosting provider (Vercel) may record basic technical information, such as IP addresses, in server logs so it can deliver and protect the site.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to talk with you and for our normal business records.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, or ask us to delete them, by emailing info@pivisen.com.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
