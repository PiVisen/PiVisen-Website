Our first product

PiLENS: an AI security camera that checks before it alerts

PiLENS runs on a small, low-cost device (Raspberry Pi 5). It spots theft and violent incidents in real time and alerts the owner. A second AI check confirms each alert first. It needs no GPU, and video stays on the premises.

Checks each 2.4-second video clip in about 0.14 seconds on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU.

Ran 2 hours live at 30 frames per second without dropping a frame.

A second AI check cut false alerts on normal test videos from 16.4% to 3.6%.

How we work

From first call to a system you can rely on

  1. Understand the problem

    We start with your goal, your site and your budget, and tell you plainly whether AI is the right tool.

  2. Prototype early

    We build a small working version first, so you can judge it on your own footage or data before you commit to more.

  3. Build for production

    We make the model, the software around it and the hardware it runs on ready for daily use.

  4. Deploy and improve

    We help you install it, watch how it performs and keep improving it as your needs change.

Edge-first by design

We make AI run on affordable devices close to the camera. That keeps costs, delays and data exposure down.

Honest numbers

We only quote results we have measured ourselves, and we tell you how we measured them.

One team, end to end

Models, back-end, apps and device set-up come from one small team, so nothing gets lost between vendors.
