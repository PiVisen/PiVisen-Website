Computer Vision & Edge AI

We teach cameras to recognise what matters to you, such as people, vehicles, products or unusual events, and we run that AI on small devices installed next to the camera. Processing on site means faster reactions, lower running costs and footage that does not have to leave the building.

A good fit if you already have cameras and want them to do more than record.

Typical work

  • Object and person detection, counting and tracking
  • Event detection with alerts
  • Making models small and fast enough for devices such as Raspberry Pi
  • Camera and device set-up

Night Surveillance Systems

Many incidents happen after dark, when ordinary cameras struggle. We build surveillance systems for low light: we choose suitable cameras and infrared lighting, prepare the images, and add AI detection tuned for night-time footage. Every system is tested on footage from your own site before it goes live.

A good fit if your site is quiet at night and you need to know when that changes.

Typical work

  • Low-light and infrared camera selection
  • Image enhancement for dark scenes
  • Intrusion and activity alerts
  • On-site testing on your night-time footage

Cloud AI & Deep Learning

Some jobs need more power than a small device can offer: training models, analysing large archives, or serving many users at once. We set up cloud pipelines to train, store and serve deep learning models, and we connect them to edge devices when a system needs both.

A good fit if you need to process a lot of images, video or data, or reach many users.

Typical work

  • Model training pipelines in the cloud
  • Model hosting behind secure APIs
  • Batch analysis of images and video
  • Hybrid set-ups: edge devices plus cloud

Machine Learning Engineering

We turn your business data into models that predict, classify or flag what needs attention, and we build the plumbing around them: data cleaning, training, testing, and a way to retrain as things change. You get a model your team can use and maintain, not a one-off experiment.

A good fit if you collect data every day but still make decisions by hand.

Typical work

  • Prediction and classification models
  • Data preparation and feature pipelines
  • Model testing and monitoring
  • Retraining workflows

Python Backend & APIs

AI is only useful when other software can talk to it. We build Python back-ends and REST APIs that connect models to your apps, websites, dashboards and databases. The code is documented and tested, so your own developers can extend it later.

A good fit if you need a reliable service behind an app, a dashboard or an AI model.

Typical work

  • REST APIs for AI models and apps
  • Database design with PostgreSQL
  • Integrations with third-party services
  • Automation scripts and background jobs

Intelligent Software Solutions

When you need a complete product rather than a single part, we bring it all together: the AI, the back-end, a web dashboard, alerts and the device set-up. We design around how your team works today, so the new system fits in instead of getting in the way.

A good fit if you have a clear problem and want one team to own the solution from start to finish.

Typical work

  • Web dashboards and admin tools
  • Alerts and notifications
  • Workflow automation
  • End-to-end AI products

How a project runs

Four steps, with a working prototype early

  1. Discovery call

    We learn your goal, your site, your data and your budget. If AI isn't the right answer, we say so.

  2. Plan and prototype

    We agree a small first version and build it, so you can see it working before you commit to more.

  3. Build

    We finish the model, software and hardware set-up, and test each part on real conditions from your site.

  4. Launch and support

    We help you go live, show your team how it works and keep improving it with you.
