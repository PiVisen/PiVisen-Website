Our first product

PiLENS: the AI security camera that checks before it alerts

PiLENS watches your camera on a small, low-cost device (Raspberry Pi 5). It detects theft and violent incidents in real time and alerts you, but only after a second AI check has confirmed the event. It needs no GPU, and your video never has to leave your premises.

Talk to us about PiLENS

What it does

Fewer false alarms. Faster response. Private by design.

Detects in real time

Spots theft and violent incidents as they happen, from your camera's live video.

Double-checks every alert

A second AI check reviews each event before you are told, so you get fewer false alarms.

No GPU needed

Runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU. No expensive graphics card and no server room.

Video stays on site

All processing happens on the device at your premises. Your footage does not go to the cloud.

Who it's for

Built for places that can't afford to miss a moment

PiLENS is designed for small and medium sites where nobody has time to watch screens all day.

Shops

Get alerted to theft on the shop floor while you serve customers.

Pharmacies

Keep an eye on high-value stock and on the safety of staff at the counter.

Offices

Know when something goes wrong at reception, in corridors or after hours.

Homes

Real-time alerts for your family, with video that stays inside your home.

How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Watch

    Your camera streams video to the PiLENS unit on site. PiLENS looks at the video in short clips, continuously.

  2. Detect and double-check

    On-device AI looks for signs of theft or violence in each clip. When it finds one, a second AI check reviews the event to rule out a false alarm.

  3. Alert

    Only confirmed events reach you, so you can act quickly and spend less time on false alarms.

Measured in our tests

The numbers behind PiLENS

Checks each 2.4-second video clip in about 0.14 seconds on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU.

Ran 2 hours live at 30 frames per second without dropping a frame.

A second AI check cut false alerts on normal test videos from 16.4% to 3.6%.
