About PiVisen AI

A small team building AI that works outside the lab

PiVisen AI is a technology start-up from Islamabad, Pakistan. We build AI products and software for businesses: computer vision, edge AI, cloud AI and the Python systems that tie them together.

  • Founded 2026
  • Islamabad, Pakistan
  • Technology company

Who we are

We believe useful AI should run on affordable hardware

A lot of AI is built for large companies with big budgets and powerful servers. Most of the people we want to help, such as shop owners, pharmacies, offices and families, have a camera, a tight budget and no time to watch screens.

So we build AI that fits them: models small enough to run on low-cost devices, software that is simple to use, and systems that keep private data where it belongs. Our first product, PiLENS, does exactly that for security cameras.

Alongside our own products, we take on client work across our six services, from a single API to a complete AI product.

What we believe

Four values that guide every project

Useful over flashy

We judge our work by the problem it solves, not by how impressive the demo looks.

Honest about limits

We tell you what AI can and cannot do for you, and we share the numbers we measured.

Privacy first

Data stays as close to its source as possible. We only move what needs to move.

Built for real hardware

We design for devices and budgets that real businesses can afford.
