Architecture

Edge, cloud or hybrid

Edge AI

The model runs on a small device next to the camera or sensor. Good for fast reactions, sites with weak internet, and video that must stay private.

  • Low running cost per site
  • Works when the internet is down
  • Footage stays on the premises

Cloud AI

The model runs on cloud servers. Good for training models, analysing large archives and serving many users from one place.

  • Power on demand for heavy jobs
  • One place to update models
  • Easy to connect to web and mobile apps

Hybrid

Devices handle the urgent, private work on site. The cloud handles training, reports and dashboards. Many real systems end up here.

  • Fast local alerts
  • Central reporting
  • Only small summaries leave the site

Engineering principles

How we keep systems useful after launch day

Privacy by design

If video does not need to leave the site, it doesn't. We keep raw footage on the device wherever possible.

Measure before we promise

We test on real conditions and report what we measured, including the cases where the system struggles.

Design for the budget

We aim for the cheapest hardware that does the job well, so a system can grow from one site to many.

Built to be handed over

Clear code, documentation and simple deployment, so your team is never locked in.
